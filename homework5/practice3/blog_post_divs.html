Understanding Web Accessibility

By Jane Doe | March 15, 2024

Web accessibility is about making websites usable for everyone, including people with disabilities. This includes users who rely on screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technologies.

Semantic HTML plays a crucial role in accessibility. When we use the correct HTML elements, we provide meaning and structure that assistive technologies can understand and communicate to users.
